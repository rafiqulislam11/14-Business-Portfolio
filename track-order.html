Our Standard 11-Stage Delivery Workflow

Every single project at RI Creative Agency is executed under a rigorous milestone process to ensure world-class quality and total alignment.

01. Quote Requested

Initial requirements submitted through our wizard or WhatsApp consultation.

02. Requirements Clarification

Detailed discussion regarding brand goals, target audience, dimensions, and styling.

03. Project Brief & Scope Lock

Deliverables list, timeline commitments, and commercial terms officially finalized.

04. Research & Strategy

Market competitor audit, typography pairings, color psychology, and moodboarding.

05. Design Draft / Structure Ready

First high-fidelity concepts, layout proofs, or functional code prototypes produced.

06. Client Review & Feedback

Client inspects the deliverables via private preview links and notes adjustments.

07. Revisions & Polishing

Incorporation of client feedback, fine-tuning kerning, color contrast, and layouts.

08. Final Quality Check (QA)

Rigorous pre-flight checklist: 300 DPI CMYK resolution, responsive audits, or KDP compliance.

09. Client Final Approval

Final sign-off by the client before file packing and commercial rights transfer.

10. Deliverables Handover

Cloud drive bundle transfer with all vector/source formats (AI, PSD, PDF, SVG, WebP).

11. Order Completed & Review

Successful handover recorded, post-launch guidance provided, and client feedback logged.
